## 80+: Strong fit

- **Eoptic, Color Scientist** (88), Rochester, NY, on-site. You'd join the Industrial Metrology
  division, which builds instruments that measure how people perceive the color, gloss and
  texture of materials. The job is to bridge physical measurement and human perception. That
  means drawing on color and appearance models, running perceptual studies with human observers,
  and prototyping metrics in MATLAB or Python. Psychophysics and display pipelines are listed as
  preferred. An MS or PhD in vision or color science is preferred, recent graduates are
  explicitly welcome, and no years of experience are required. The caveats: no salary is listed,
  and the page shows no posting date, so **its age is unknown**. The application form is live.
  Eoptic isn't on the watchlist and has no ATS (it uses a form on its own site), so it can't be
  added for automatic checks.

  preferred. You've said you want to avoid psychometrics, and that's half this job, so it's only
  worth applying if the people analytics side appeals. It's also a manager-level role. Pay is
  $110k to $165.3k, with no visa sponsorship. Posted Nov 4.
