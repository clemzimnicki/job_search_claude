## 80+: Strong fit

  $159.5K-$236.5K, posted Oct 5. This is a mixed-methods researcher on the Enterprise Services
  Experience Research team, which covers businesses, marketplaces, and partners. You would own
  research programs end to end, from roadmaps and study design (surveys, usability testing, data
  analysis) to vendor-run studies, and you would carry findings into design and product
  workshops. The posting asks researchers to bring in new tools, including AI methods, which
  earned the AI bonus. The stretch is the Staff title: it asks for 5+ years and prefers 7+ with

  Austin is 15 days old, so it was skipped.
