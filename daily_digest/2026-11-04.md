## 80+: Strong fit

- **Microsoft, Principal UX Researcher, AI Experiences** (86), Mountain View or Redmond, in
  office four days a week. You'd lead mixed-methods research on Copilot Cowork, the agent that
  takes multi-step tasks off people's hands. The quant side is surveys with proper sampling and
  power, experiments, segmentation, and mixed-effects or driver models on telemetry. The posting
  also asks you to help run side-by-side agent evaluations: building the task sets, rubrics and
  analysis plans. That lines up closely with your experimental design and crowdsourcing work.
  The catch is level. It's a principal (IC5) role, and the minimum is a PhD plus 3 years of UX
  research. The preferred bar is 5 years plus experience researching AI products, so expect
  questions about seniority. You'd also be expected to use SQL or KQL on telemetry. Pay is
  $142.8k to $274.8k. The req was created Oct 30 (the board shows a refreshed date of today),
  and it's still accepting applications.
