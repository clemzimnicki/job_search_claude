**Project 5 (2024-2025)**
- Led a combined online/in-person behavioral study (Prolific/UW-Madison SONA). PI: Timothy T. Rogers.
- Contributions: stimulus design (Adobe Photoshop), programmed experiment using
  JsPsych/JavaScript, analyzed data (R), visualizations (R/ggplot), wrote abstract, poster for
  VSS (Adobe Illustrator).
- Outcome: abstract published in Cognitive Science Society proceedings, poster at CogSci 2025.
- Study: collected perceptual similarity judgments for 58 colors via triadic comparisons,
  embedded into personalized 3D spaces per participant. Found reliable individual differences
  in perceived color similarity, and two structured "motifs" (distinct-category cluster vs.
  continuous-space cluster) rather than random variation.

**Project 6 (2025-2026)**
- Led 3 online behavioral studies (Prolific + UW-Madison SONA). PI: Timothy T. Rogers.
- Contributions: stimulus design (Adobe Photoshop), programmed experiment using
  JsPsych/JavaScript, analyzed data (R), visualizations (R/ggplot); writing results in
  dissertation.
- Outcome: abstract published in CogSci proceedings; part of dissertation work.
- Study: extended triadic-comparisons individual-differences work from color to 2D line
  orientation (Gabor patches). Experiment 1: people judged similarity by orientation, no
  clustering. Experiment 2: people judged similarity by contrast (ignoring orientation), no
  clustering. Experiment 3: with explicit orientation instructions in part 1, people clustered
  into orientation- vs. contrast-based groups; in part 2 (no instructions) everyone reverted to
  contrast.

**Project 7 (2025-2026)**
- Led an online behavioral study (UW-Madison SONA). PI: Timothy T. Rogers.
- Contributions: stimulus design (Adobe Photoshop), programmed experiment using
  JsPsych/JavaScript, analyzed data (R), visualizations (R/ggplot); writing results in
  dissertation.
- Study: applied triadic comparisons to 2D moving stimuli (dot groups / RDKs, based on Emery
  et al. 2023). Found individual differences but no clustering.

**Project 8 (2026): Do individual differences in perceived color similarity reflect category generalization?**
- Currently leading an in-person behavioral study (UW-Madison SONA). PI: Timothy T. Rogers.
- Contributions: stimulus design (MATLAB), programmed experiment using JsPsych/JavaScript
  (Claude Code-assisted), calibrated monitors using photoradiometer, analyzing data (R),
  visualizations (R/ggplot), writing results in dissertation.
- Study: pairs the triadic-comparisons task with a color categorization-generalization task to
  test whether individual color-space embeddings predict how category learning generalizes.

**Project 9 (2026): Are individual differences in perceived color similarity a result of strategy or ability?**
- Currently leading an in-person behavioral study (UW-Madison SONA). PI: Timothy T. Rogers.
- Contributions: stimulus design (MATLAB), programmed experiment using JsPsych/JavaScript
  (Claude Code-assisted), calibrated monitors using photoradiometer, analyzing data (R),
  visualizations (R/ggplot), writing results in dissertation.
- Study: compares triplet-task response patterns to performance on a Garner interference task
  (color hue vs. lightness classification) to test whether individual differences reflect
  strategy (changeable at will) or ability (fixed).
